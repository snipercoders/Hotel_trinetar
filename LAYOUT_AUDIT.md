# Layout audit

1. The hero is full-bleed but places its headline and booking form low in the frame; the primary action competes with the image and arrives late in the visual scan.
2. Nearly every section repeats the same centered eyebrow/title, rounded image cards, and three/four-column grid, so the page has little change in pace or emphasis.
3. The stays, experiences, reviews, and gallery all rely on image/card collections, creating a long run of similar visual units instead of a clear editorial story.
4. Large vertical padding and tall cards stretch the page, while the fixed header and hero booking fields consume valuable space on a 375px screen.
5. The booking path is repeated in the header, hero, and final CTA but has no persistent, compact action after the hero; visitors must scroll back or wait until the end.
